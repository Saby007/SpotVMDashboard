How to read these results

Spot VMs can be interrupted when Azure reclaims spare capacity or your maximum price is exceeded. Eviction history uses the last 28 trailing days for a SKU and region, not an individual zone or a guarantee of future uptime.

Quota is your permitted vCPU allowance, not physical capacity. Each score applies only to the requested Spot VM count, size, region, zone, subscription, and time. A low score does not prevent deployment, and a high score does not reserve capacity. Regular VMs use different capacity and quota rules. Azure validates the actual deployment.
